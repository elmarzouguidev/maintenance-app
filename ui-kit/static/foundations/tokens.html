Design tokens

Colours, type scale, radius, elevation and motion: the only values a view may use.

Brand (brand/brand.json)

Utilities: bg-*, text-*, border-*, outline-*, fill-* with the token name. Raw palette colours (slate, blue…) and arbitrary values are forbidden in views.

  • brand Brand colour: platform chip, avatars, primary stat marker
  • brand-deep Darkest brand shade: platform context band, overlay backdrops
  • accent Primary action, focus ring, selection, current item rail. Nothing else
  • accent-hover Primary action on hover
  • accent-soft Selected card or option background
  • accent-strong Text on accent-soft
  • on-accent Text and icons on accent, brand and status fills
  • focus Focus outline
  • sidebar Tenant sidebar and drawer
  • sidebar-text Sidebar links
  • sidebar-muted Sidebar group labels
  • sidebar-rail Current-page rail
  • sidebar-focus Focus outline on the sidebar
  • sidebar-strong Current and hovered sidebar text
  • sidebar-hover Sidebar link hover (translucent)
  • sidebar-active Current sidebar link (translucent)

Surfaces and text

  • canvas Page background
  • surface Panels, cards, topbar, inputs
  • surface-muted Table headers, hovers, insets, read-only inputs
  • line Hairlines and panel borders
  • line-strong Control borders (at least 3:1 on surface and canvas)
  • fg Main text
  • fg-muted Secondary text, labels, help

Status (meaning only)

  • status-success Done, paid, available
  • status-success-soft Success badge and alert background
  • status-success-strong Text on success-soft
  • status-warning Attention soon
  • status-warning-soft Warning badge and alert background
  • status-warning-strong Text on warning-soft
  • status-danger Wrong or act now; destructive actions
  • status-danger-soft Danger badge and alert background
  • status-danger-strong Text on danger-soft
  • status-info Scheduled or informational
  • status-info-soft Info badge and alert background
  • status-info-strong Text on info-soft
  • status-neutral Inactive, cancelled, draft
  • status-neutral-soft Neutral badge background
  • status-neutral-strong Text on neutral-soft

Charts (series identity only, fixed order)

  • chart-1 Series 1
  • chart-2 Series 2
  • chart-3 Series 3 (below 3:1: label it)
  • chart-4 Series 4
  • chart-5 Series 5 (below 3:1: label it)
  • chart-6 Series 6 (below 3:1: label it)

Radius, borders and elevation

Panels have a hairline border and no shadow. The overlay shadow is for menus, popovers, dialogs and sheets only. 6 px controls, 10 px panels, full for badges and avatars.
